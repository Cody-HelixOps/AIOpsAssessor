The path diagram uses a finer wedge: Siloed, Consolidated, Correlated, Predictive, Autonomous. In the meeting, stay with the three profiles above unless you are looking at that diagram together. The last question asks how ready they are for agentic AI (HelixGPT agents that investigate and act). If they are wary of the word “agent,” talk about trusted data and governed automation. A finished run cannot be a 1 — six answers are at least 6 points, and 6 points is a Level 2. Bookmark the score you’re prepping, for example sales.html#6.

How the number is calculated

Five questions score 1–4. Agentic AI readiness scores 1–5. Maximum is 25. The ring shows round(points ÷ 25 × 10), and at least 1. That is the same formula as the results page.

Every question is worth at least 1, so a finished assessment is 6–25 points and lands on levels 2–10. Levels below 2 only exist if the run didn’t finish.
